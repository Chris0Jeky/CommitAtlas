# Code of conduct

CommitAtlas is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing another person's credentials or data.
- Accept maintainers' moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior promptly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

For sensitive conduct concerns, open a minimal issue requesting private contact and do not include the incident details. Use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools as well when the behavior violates GitHub policy. Non-sensitive moderation requests may be reported directly in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior is harmful or unsafe. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as the available GitHub tooling permits. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and applies in all CommitAtlas community spaces.
